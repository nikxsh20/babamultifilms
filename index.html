About Us

Precision shrink films, made in India for the world.

Headquartered in Delhi, Baba Multifilms LLP specializes in LDPE and LLDPE shrink films for domestic and export markets. From single-SKU bundling rolls to multi-gauge film for automated shrink tunnels, our facility is built to deliver consistent, export-grade film backed by disciplined quality control.

We are built to serve customers across India and international markets with flexible order sizes, custom widths, and gauges tailored to your packaging line.
